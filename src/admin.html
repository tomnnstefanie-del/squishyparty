Bookings & referrals

How to use this dashboard
  1. A request comes in. It appears in Telegram and here as 🟡 Waiting for vendors, with a to-do for each partner (Butter Studio, Bloom…). Reply to the customer on WhatsApp and tap ✅ Contacted in Telegram: that starts your response-time clock.
  2. Check each vendor. Ask each partner if they can do the date. When one says yes, tap 🤝 [Vendor] confirmed in Telegram (or Vendor confirmed on the booking card).
  3. All vendors confirmed. The booking turns 🟠 Ask for deposit by itself (also when no partner add-ons were chosen). WhatsApp the customer to check they're happy to pay the deposit.
  4. Deposit received. Tap 💵 Deposit paid. The booking turns 🔵 Deposit paid: the date is booked, so schedule your team from the calendar.
  5. Balance received. Tap 💰 Paid in full. The booking turns 🟢 Paid in full and leaves the to-do list.
  6. They don't go ahead. Tap Didn't book on the card; it turns grey and drops off the calendar.

Every morning at 9am, Telegram gets a 📋 to-do list of everything still pending, with one button per booking for its next step. Tap it when that step is done and this dashboard updates. Need it now? Use Send to-do list to Telegram at the top.

Tapped the wrong button? Open Details on the booking and click the ticked button to undo it. Every tap is timed and shows under Activity. Only requests sent after the database was connected appear here.

    How each number is worked out
    • Paid party: a request in the window with a deposit recorded. New customer: a paid party not flagged as a returning customer (same email or WhatsApp as an earlier booking).
    • Reached the site = visits (once per browser session) + QR scans + friend-link opens. Conversion at each step = this step ÷ the step before, so you can see where people drop off.
    • Touchpoints: retail store QR (?src=outlet), capsule QR (one generic code), search / social / direct (everything else), friend’s WhatsApp referral link (each family’s own code).
    • Average revenue per party = quoted value of paid parties ÷ paid parties (split into squishy activity and add-ons).
    • Contribution per party = revenue − kits (cost × participants) − facilitator staff − partner costs (partner add-ons × partner %) − $10 discount − free-squishy reward.
    • Gross profit per party = revenue − kits − staff − partner costs. This is the value of one customer (most families book once a year).
    • CAC = all marketing costs ÷ new customers. By touchpoint: that touchpoint’s cost ÷ its new customers. Capsule = sticker printing + $10 discounts; friend link = $10 discounts + rewards; retail = QR printing; search / social = ad spend.
    • LTV : CAC = gross profit per party ÷ CAC. 3 : 1 or more means each $1 spent on marketing brings back $3 of profit.
    • Marketing ROI = (gross profit of paid parties − marketing cost) ÷ marketing cost. Marketing cost = printing + ads + programme staff + other + $10 discounts + rewards.
    • Viral coefficient (K) = friends’ paid parties ÷ other paid parties. K of 0.2 means every 5 customers bring 1 more for free.
    • Referral rate = parties paid in full whose family link brought at least one friend’s paid party ÷ parties paid in full. Reward redemption = vouchers redeemed ÷ vouchers sent.
    • Company yearly rate = company requests that ticked “every year” ÷ company requests. Rebooking = company requests from returning clients ÷ company requests.
