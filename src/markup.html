How Squishy Party began

Squishy Party started at the squishy bar in our Tom & Stefanie stores, where children make their own squishies from scratch. Bringing it to birthday parties was the natural next step. But a party needs much more: a cake, food, styling and entertainment, each from a different vendor. So we partnered with vendors we trust and put it all into one plan, one quote and one team, so you can enjoy the party instead of organising it.

What happens on the day, and what to have ready.

  1. We arrive 15 minutes early

    Our team sets up before your start time, then runs 45 minutes of guided squishy making. We’re on site for up to 2 hours.

  2. Tell us exactly where

    Add the exact spot, like “Clubhouse II, function room level 2”, so our team finds you straight away.

  3. Have the space ready

    You provide the venue, plus tables and chairs for the children. We bring all the materials, trays and tools.

  4. A moment for the birthday girl

    We bring a sparkly Birthday Girl sash and tiara for her to wear during the party, and take them back afterwards.

  5. Every child takes one home

    Each child decorates a squishy capsule with rhinestone stickers, and their squishy goes home in it.

  6. Food and cake

    We don’t cater food, so please arrange it. Add a Butter Studio cake in the planner, or bring your own.

Are squishies safe? The facts, and when to take care.

Myth

“All squishies are toxic.”

Fact

The well-known 2018 warning from Denmark was about mass-produced foam squishies, which gave off solvents such as dimethylformamide, styrene and toluene. Ours aren’t foam squishies off a shelf: each child fills a soft squishy with their own toppings, and our team seals it for them.

Myth

“Slime is full of borax.”

Fact

Boron is what makes many slimes stretchy, and the amount is what matters. EU toy rules cap boron in toy slime at 300 mg per kg, and tests have found some cheap slimes well over that. It’s a good reason to choose slime from a supplier you trust, rather than mixing your own with laundry products.

Myth

“Small parts are fine if it’s a toy.”

Fact

Charms, beads and sprinkles are small parts, and children under 3 are most at risk of choking on them. That’s why our hosts stay at the table and guide every step, and why we ask you to keep toddlers away from the toppings.

How we keep it safe

  • Our hosts guide and watch every step, from choosing toppings to the finished squishy.
  • Our team helps fill and securely ties each squishy, so nothing comes loose.
  • Every squishy goes home sealed in its own capsule.

When to take care

  • Under-3s: keep babies and toddlers, including younger siblings, away from the toppings table.
  • Not food: some toppings look like sweets. Remind children not to put them in their mouths.
  • Wash hands after making and playing.
  • If a squishy tears or leaks, throw it away, and don’t let children bite or chew it.
  • Allergies or sensitive skin: tell us when you request, and stop and wash if skin gets red or itchy.
  • If a child swallows a part and seems unwell, get medical help straight away.
How much does Squishy DIY cost?

Jelly is $16, Crunchy $18 and the Duo $26 per child, plus $2 per child for the take-home squishy capsule and a one-time $150 travel & set-up fee. The minimum is 10 children.

Which Squishy DIY options are there?

There are three. Duo: each child makes one Jelly and one Crunchy squishy. Jelly: one smooth, clear squishy filled with charms. Crunchy: one squishy packed with crunchy toppings. Every option includes a take-home squishy capsule to decorate with rhinestone stickers, all materials and our hosts. For a birthday girl, we also bring a sparkly Birthday Girl sash and tiara to wear during the party (we take them back afterwards).

How are vendors priced?

Most add-ons show their price as you plan. Cake prices depend on size and flavour, so we add them to your quotation.

Who coordinates the vendors?

We do. We confirm each vendor for your date, arrange timings and delivery, and send you one quotation.

Can I arrange my own cake or food?

Yes. We don’t cater food, so your to-do list reminds you to arrange it. If you’re bringing your own cake, just skip Cakes in the planner.

Can companies book?

Yes: team bonding workshops, family days and booths at roadshows or client events. Prices are the same as shown, per person. When you send your request, choose “A company” and we’ll send a quotation and invoice once we confirm, instead of a PayNow deposit.

What does Wax Cracking cost?

$2 per squishy for Jelly or Crunchy. With the Duo, it’s $50 for every 20 squishies (U.P. $60).

How long is the activity?

One hour of our time: 15 minutes to set up, then 45 minutes of guided play. The travel & set-up fee covers our team for up to 2 hours on site.

What do I need to provide?

The venue, tables and chairs, and anything you don’t add from our partners, such as food or cake. Your checklist updates as you plan.

When do I pay?

When you send your request, you pay a 10% holding deposit on your Squishy Party by PayNow. We then confirm availability on WhatsApp and send one quotation. The deposit is refunded in full if we’re unable to attend on your date, and deducted from your full amount if you go ahead with your package. It’s non-refundable if you cancel.
